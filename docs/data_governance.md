# Research Ethics and Data Governance

## Purpose limitation
The project evaluates text-processing systems for research. It does not provide legal advice, assess the enforceability of clauses, or make decisions about parties or contracts.

## Source provenance
Record the origin, retrieval date, version, and applicable terms for every dataset and document collection. CUAD and SEC EDGAR materials are distinct sources; do not assume their licenses or reuse conditions are interchangeable.

## Public repository policy
Do not commit:
- Raw contract files or full extracted contract text unless redistribution permission has been verified.
- Credentials, API keys, access tokens, or private configuration files.
- Personal contact details, private correspondence, or account identifiers.
- Private sampling manifests that reveal unnecessary sensitive information.
- Notebook outputs containing restricted source text or personal data.

Use synthetic examples for tests and documentation. Keep any required private mapping between project IDs and source documents outside the public repository.

## Model use
Treat model predictions as research artifacts. Do not use them to make decisions about actual legal rights, obligations, counterparties, or disputes. Document model limitations and version changes.

## Review gate
Before releasing any dataset, notebook, paper, or trained artifact, review source terms, privacy, provenance, and third-party license obligations.
