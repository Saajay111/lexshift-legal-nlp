# Provisional Hypotheses

These hypotheses are expectations, not findings. Revise them after the literature review and pilot, recording the reason and date.

- **H1 — Cross-domain performance difference:** At least some model/category combinations will exhibit a measurable difference between held-out CUAD contracts and the later SEC target sample.
- **H2 — Category heterogeneity:** The size and direction of the difference will vary across the five CUAD-aligned categories.
- **H3 — Drafting-feature association:** Some error types will be associated with pre-defined drafting features such as nonstandard headings, cross-references, or provisions distributed across sections.
- **H4 — Model trade-offs:** More complex models may outperform lexical baselines on some categories but may not eliminate target-domain performance differences.

Specify metrics, comparisons, analysis units, and uncertainty procedures before accessing final target test labels. A non-significant result is not proof of no difference.
