# Provisional Hypotheses

These hypotheses are testable expectations, not results. Revise them after the literature review and pilot, documenting the reason and date.

- **H1 — Generalization gap:** At least some model/category combinations will exhibit a measurable source-to-target performance difference.
- **H2 — Category heterogeneity:** The size and direction of the difference will vary across clause categories.
- **H3 — Drafting association:** Some error types will be associated with pre-defined drafting features, such as nonstandard headings, provisions distributed across sections, or cross-references.
- **H4 — Model trade-offs:** More complex models may outperform lexical baselines on some categories but may not eliminate target-domain performance differences.

For each hypothesis, specify the metric, comparison, analysis unit, and uncertainty procedure before opening the locked target test labels. A non-significant result is not proof that no difference exists.
