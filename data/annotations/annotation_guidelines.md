# Annotation Guidelines — Draft v0.1

**Status:** Draft for pilot use only. Do not begin main-study annotation until the pilot is complete and protocol v1.0 is frozen.

## 1. Purpose
Create consistent labels for selected clause categories in SEC-filed material contracts. These definitions are research operationalizations, not legal advice.

## 2. Unit of annotation
The proposed unit is a clause passage: the smallest contiguous passage that expresses the relevant provision while retaining its qualifications. If a provision spans multiple paragraphs, include all necessary text and record offsets against a versioned normalized document.

## 3. Working category definitions
- **Governing law:** A provision specifying the law governing interpretation or enforcement of the agreement.
- **Termination:** A provision specifying when or how the agreement or a party's obligations may expire or be terminated.
- **Limitation of liability:** A provision limiting, excluding, capping, or allocating liability or damages.
- **Non-compete:** A provision restricting a party from specified competitive activities, subject to a final scope definition.
- **Anti-assignment:** A provision restricting or conditioning assignment, transfer, or delegation of contractual rights or obligations.

Verify these definitions against CUAD documentation and pilot examples before freezing the codebook.

## 4. Decision rules
1. Label text supported by the agreement, not an annotator's assumptions.
2. A heading alone is not sufficient evidence.
3. Include exceptions and qualifications that are part of the provision.
4. A passing reference to a concept is not necessarily a clause.
5. Record cross-references when a provision depends on another section.
6. Mark ambiguous examples for review rather than guessing.
7. Apply multiple labels only when the text independently satisfies multiple operational definitions.

## 5. Quality assurance
Pilot a small sample, discuss disagreements, revise the guide, and freeze version 1.0 before main annotation. If a second annotator is available, have them independently label a subset. Report raw agreement and an appropriate statistic, along with category prevalence. Keep original labels, adjudication decisions, and rationale in separate fields.
